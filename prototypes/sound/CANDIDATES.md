## Notes

- Gong #9 is a Japanese temple bell (bonsho), included as an extra long-ring reference, not a gong. Gong #8 is the closest thing to a gamelan gong found; no CC0 recording of a Vietnamese cong chieng or chau gong turned up (searches: gong ageng, kempul, gamelan gong, chau gong, cong chieng, Vietnamese gong returned nothing usable).
- Horn #1 to #3 are conch or snail horns, #4 and #5 shofars, the rest are brass or wooden horns. No CC0 buffalo horn turned up.
- Several drum clips are multi-hit loops, rolls or ensembles (#1 to #6, #8 to #10, #13); the audition page detects the hits in each so the three moments can reuse one hit or a roll from any of them.
